# Warranty Policy

## Coverage
Electronics carry the manufacturer warranty stated on the product page, usually 6 to 12 months from the delivery date.
Warranty covers manufacturing defects only.

## Not covered
Water damage, physical damage, unauthorized repairs, and normal wear of batteries and ear cushions are not covered.

## Claims
To claim warranty the customer needs the order ID and a description of the problem.
Warranty claims are always handled by a human support agent, so the AI assistant should escalate them.
