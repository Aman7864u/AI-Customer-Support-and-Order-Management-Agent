# Delivery and Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within metro cities and 5 to 8 business days elsewhere.
Estimated delivery dates are shown on the order and may shift due to weather, strikes, or carrier delays.

## Delayed deliveries
An order is considered delayed when it has not arrived by the estimated delivery date.
- Delayed by 1 to 3 days: apologize, share the latest tracking status, and ask the customer to wait.
- Delayed by more than 3 days: the customer is entitled to a human follow-up. Escalate with priority MEDIUM.
- Delayed by more than 7 days: customer may choose a full refund or a re-shipment. Escalate with priority HIGH.

## Lost packages
If tracking has shown no movement for 5 business days, the package is treated as potentially lost.
Escalate to a human agent with priority HIGH. A replacement or refund is arranged after the carrier investigation.

## Failed delivery attempts
The carrier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. After that the package returns to the warehouse and the
customer is refunded or re-shipped on request.

## Shipping charges
Orders above 999 INR ship free. Orders below that carry a flat 49 INR shipping fee, which is not refundable
unless the return is due to a defective or wrong item.

## Tracking
Tracking numbers are shared once an order reaches SHIPPED status. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear.
