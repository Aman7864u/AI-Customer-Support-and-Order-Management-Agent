# Refund and Return Policy

## Return window
Customers can request a return within 7 days of the delivery date for most products.
Products must be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories and the invoice.

## Non-returnable items
Opened software, gift cards, and items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.
Products with physical damage caused by the customer are not eligible for a refund.

## Defective or wrong items
If the item arrives defective, damaged, or different from what was ordered, the customer can request a
replacement or a full refund within 7 days of delivery. Photos of the item and packaging may be requested.

## Refund processing
Once a returned item is received and inspected, the refund is issued to the original payment method
within 5 to 7 business days. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer after the customer
shares bank details with a human support agent.

## Refund approval
Refunds above 10,000 INR, refunds requested after the return window, and refunds for opened items
must be approved by a human support agent. The AI assistant cannot approve these refunds and should escalate.

## Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled for a full refund while the status is PROCESSING. Once an order is SHIPPED,
it cannot be cancelled and must follow the return process after delivery.
